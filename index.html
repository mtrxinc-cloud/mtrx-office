Questions

Good to know.

Will my files open in Microsoft Word?

Yes. Writer saves standard .docx files, and the PDF app edits the PDF itself, so other programs open them normally.

What data do you collect?

None. MTRX Office has no accounts, no tracking and no analytics. Your documents stay on your computer and are never uploaded. The only network use is checking for and downloading updates.

Do I need internet?

Only to download the app and to receive updates. Writing, editing and saving work fully offline.

How do updates work?

The app checks for new versions and installs signed updates. You always keep your own files and settings.

Can I make the toolbar and menus my own?

Yes. Open View, then Customize toolbar. Drag menus, tabs and toolbar groups into the order you like, change their size, and hide buttons. Your choices are saved.

Is it free?

You can download and try it now. Pricing will be announced once the apps are more complete.

Where do I get help?

Open Help in the app for a quick guide and shortcuts. Support and feedback with an MTRX account are coming.
